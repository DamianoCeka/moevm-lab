# Privacy notice for repository inquiries

Last updated: 2026-08-14

This notice applies to public commercial-pilot inquiries submitted to the
MoEVM Lab GitHub repository. It complements the
[GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement),
which governs GitHub's operation of the platform.

## Who handles the inquiry

Damiano Ceka, the repository owner and maintainer, reviews commercial-pilot
inquiries. Until a dedicated private commercial address is published, contact
is through the [DamianoCeka GitHub profile](https://github.com/DamianoCeka) and
the relevant repository issue. Do not submit an inquiry if that public contact
path is not appropriate for you.

## Data involved and purpose

Opening an issue exposes the GitHub account, profile information, timestamps
and content that the submitter chooses to provide. The maintainer uses that
information only to:

- understand the requested technical decision;
- assess whether a fit review, pilot or integration discussion is feasible;
- respond to the request and, when appropriate, take steps requested before a
  possible written engagement;
- protect the repository from abuse and preserve an auditable project record.

The form does not request an email address, legal name, billing information,
credentials, private weights or confidential customer material. Do not add
those items voluntarily.

For reviewing the inquiry and taking requested steps before a possible
engagement, the intended legal basis is Article 6(1)(b) GDPR. For repository
security, abuse prevention and the minimum audit record needed to operate the
public project, the intended basis is Article 6(1)(f): the maintainer's
legitimate interest in protecting and administering the repository. No
automated decision-making or profiling is used. These bases should be reviewed
if the intake process or the type of information collected changes.

## Public visibility and recipients

The issue, GitHub username and submitted content are public. They may be read,
indexed, copied or cached by GitHub, search engines and other third parties.
GitHub processes platform data under its own privacy terms. No public issue is
a confidential channel, and the maintainer cannot control third-party copies
or caches.

Inquiry information is not sold or added to an unrelated marketing list. If a
private follow-up is mutually agreed, its access, retention and deletion rules
must be defined separately before private material is shared.

## Retention and requests

An inactive inquiry that does not proceed is closed after 90 days and should be
reviewed for deletion no later than 12 months after its last activity. A record
may be kept longer when needed for an active engagement, a legal obligation or
the establishment, exercise or defence of legal claims. Records moved into a
private engagement are retained under its written terms and applicable
accounting or legal requirements.

A submitter may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or, where the
legitimate-interest basis applies, object to processing. Make the request by
mentioning `@DamianoCeka` on the relevant issue without posting new personal or
confidential information. Requests will be assessed together with applicable
legal obligations and GitHub's available controls. The submitter may also
[contact or complain to the Italian data-protection authority](https://www.garanteprivacy.it/it/i-miei-diritti).
Deletion from the repository cannot guarantee removal from third-party archives
or caches.

If this public process is unsuitable, do not submit the form. Wait for a
separately agreed private channel and written scope.
